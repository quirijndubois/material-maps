<p>A small, fast maps app built with Material You. It runs on open data only: no Google Play Services, no accounts, no API keys, no tracking.</p>

<p>Search places, get driving, cycling, walking and public transport directions, follow turn-by-turn navigation, and check live departures at any stop or station. Public transport works best in the Netherlands, with live data for NS, GVB, RET, HTM, Arriva, Qbuzz and others.</p>

<p><b>Map</b></p>
<ul>
<li>OpenStreetMap vector map from OpenFreeMap with 3D buildings, shops, cafés, sights and transit stops</li>
<li>Light and dark themes; the dark map is recoloured with your wallpaper colours</li>
<li>Layers: satellite and earlier years' aerial photos, terrain, contour lines, rain radar, clouds and lightning of the last hours, wind, cloud, storm and air quality forecasts (including CO₂ and methane) with a time slider, measured air quality, train, metro, tram and bus lines, the rail network, hiking, cycling, mountain biking and ski routes, sea marks, and pins for charging, drinking water, toilets, AEDs, bike parking, bike repair and Wikipedia articles</li>
<li>For the Netherlands: elevation (AHN) and land parcels (Kadaster)</li>
<li>Live location with a smooth heading arrow, and a follow mode that rotates the map with your phone</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Search</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Results as you type, biased toward what's on screen</li>
<li>Show all results on the map as pins</li>
<li>Coordinates, recent places, and tap or long-press anything on the map</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Directions</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Up to three routes for driving, cycling and walking, with a step-by-step list</li>
<li>Turn-by-turn navigation with automatic rerouting</li>
<li>Public transport journeys with line colours, platforms, live delays and cancellations</li>
<li>Hand the destination off to another navigation app</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Departures</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Live departure boards for every stop and station, with a tab per kind of transport</li>
</ul>

<p>Data and services: map tiles from OpenFreeMap, routing by Valhalla on the FOSSGIS server, public transport from Transitous (MOTIS), search fallback from Photon by komoot, satellite photos from Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX, with aerial photos of the Netherlands from Beeldmateriaal Nederland (PDOK), place details and pins from Nominatim and the Overpass API, bus routes from Transitous, descriptions from Wikipedia, elevation from Terrain Tiles and AHN (PDOK), parcels from the Kadaster (PDOK), rain radar from RainViewer, clouds and lightning © EUMETSAT, weather forecasts from Open-Meteo, air quality forecasts from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (drawn by ECMWF), measured air quality from Luchtmeetnet and Sensor.Community, and overlays from Waymarked Trails, OpenRailwayMap, OpenSnowMap, OpenSeaMap and VersaTiles. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.</p>
